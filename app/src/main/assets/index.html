For healthcare professionals

Oncology Pulse is an education and awareness tool for doctors and trainees. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for clinical judgement.

  • News summaries are compiled automatically each day from regulators, journals, companies and medical news sites, with a link to each original source.
  • Landmark trial summaries are a revision aid and may contain errors.
  • Always confirm figures, indications and doses against the original publication or prescribing information before using them for patient care.

The app collects no personal data. Saved items stay on your phone.
